**An operating workbook for the 2026–2027 thesis**

Volume I / II — Foundation layer · v1.0 · 2026-05-20

This workbook is the consolidated substrate for the next 18 months of strategic bets. Seven chapters and five plates replace what was previously a four-document pile of `AI_STACK_REPORT.docx`, `AI_STACK_ADDENDUM.docx`, `OCQ_TRACKER.md`, and two HANDOVER drafts. The markdown source is the canonical editable layer; this EPUB is the regenerable read-anywhere export.

Downstream artifacts: Volume III (`output/ai-agents/`) zooms into the agent layer at higher resolution; Volume IV (`output/agents-gtm/`) is the GTM playbook that builds on both.

For taxonomy, see `_spec.md`. For update protocol, see Chapter 6 §6.4. For the rituals that keep this alive, see Chapter 5.
