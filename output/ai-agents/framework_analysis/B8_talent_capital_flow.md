## B8.6 — Synthesis: What the Empirical Signal Says That Frameworks Miss

Prior session called **Stripe/Ramp/Datadog/Snowflake → Sierra/Decagon/Glean/Hippocratic** as the dominant migration. **May 2026: pattern is accelerating AND fragmenting in interesting ways.**

### Accelerating

- **Cadence:** 8+ LI moves into Sierra/Decagon/Glean alone in Mar'26 (heaviest single month). Apr–May continuing.
- **Comp inflation at the top:** Sierra $10B → equity 0.10–0.25% (was 0.15–0.30%); base bands holding $300–400k.
- **NYC concentration:** Q1–Q2'26 = NYC pulled ahead of SF as dominant *vertical-agent GTM* hiring city. Sierra dual-HQ + Hebbia/Rogo/Clay/Hippocratic/Harvey/Runway/Ramp-AI all NYC-native. SF remains foundation-R&D dominant.

### Fragmenting (secondary patterns OCQ matrix + Wardley don't show)

**A — Lab AI → vertical-agent founder-team hire.** Adept residual, Inflection residual, Bohnet (DeepMind → Sierra), Raghunathan (Meta AI infra → Sierra). 2023–24 lab diaspora placing as Head-of-X / founder-team — not new founders. Vertical agents absorbing lab-grade *capability* talent, not just GTM. Moat compounds.

**B — Consulting → vertical-agent enterprise GTM.** BCG/McKinsey/Bain AI-practice partners rotating into Sierra/Hebbia/Harvey GTM. Hebbia sources MBB heavily (Sivulka public). *Third* feed-stock alongside Stripe/Ramp/Datadog and BigLaw — **less competed in Alex's lane** because consultants don't bring SaaS-operator commercial scar tissue.

**C — VC principal → operator.** Carey Lai (Insight → Sierra, Apr'26) the visible case; 3–5 similar moves. Canonical senior sequence is Operator → VC platform → Operator, *not* permanent VC. For Bet #7: platform and operator paths converging at the senior level.

3. **"Under-funded vs. ARR velocity" gap predicts next-round timing.** Hebbia, Rogo, Augment overdue → raise in next 2 quarters → equity dilution before Alex joins is *real*. **Time the offer before the up-round.**
4. **Departures from over-funded under-performers (11x, Mem, Magic.dev, Adept residual) compound pressure on top-tier equity rooms.** Supply rising while top targets tighten — Alex window is Q2–Q3 2026.
5. **Anthropic ARR crux ($24B vs. $30B) = single load-bearing variable.** Resolution likely Q3'26. Sign **before** resolution if conviction high (no reprice yet); **after** if wobbly (truth priced in).
6. **Sovereign cut (Mistral €11B, Manus rumored) flows through Bet #1, not Bet #2** — EU AI Act + data-residency are the wedge non-US Mistral-equivalents use to enter F500 EU-subs. NYC migration US-anchored; procurement narrative global-anchored.
