## 6. Section D — Operator-Shape Powers Alex Can Claim + Mapping to Bets

Alex cannot claim Scale, Network, or Cornered Resource on his own. He CAN claim three operator-shape flavors:

**(i) Process Power — operational scar tissue.** 12 years enterprise procurement + AI-builder fluency = rare-profile. The work of running 50+ F1000 AI procurement reviews is not codifiable in a runbook; the seven-counterparty workflow (InfoSec / Legal / Privacy / AI Governance / Procurement / Sponsor / EA) is the operational craft. **Anchored on Bet #1.** Strengthens with each Playbook engagement; compounds across cycles.

**(ii) Branding — canonical voice.** The Playbook publication moves Alex from "operator" to "the procurement-standard maintainer." First-credible-publisher locks the procurement default for next 5 years (Playbook §III.5 Signed Reproducible Eval; §III.6 Article 14 Tie-Out — both at Genesis stage May'26). **Anchored on Bet #1 + Bet #6.** Newsletter (Bet#6) is the distribution layer; Playbook is the artifact.

**(iii) Downstream Switching Costs — buyer-side reference standard.** If the Playbook becomes the buyer-side procurement reference, every AI vendor MUST answer to it (rubric vocabulary, addendum library, evaluation format). Switching Costs *flow downstream*: F500 procurement teams won't rewrite the rubric; vendors won't repropose against an unfamiliar one. **Anchored on Bet #1 → Bet #3 (gateway advisory) compounds.**

**Bet-by-Bet mapping:**

- **Bet #1 (Procurement Playbook):** Builds (i) Process Power (anchor), (ii) Branding (anchor), (iii) Switching Costs (anchor downstream). **Highest power-density bet in the portfolio — three flavors stacked.** Falsifiable on Vanta/Drata/Ironclad AI-vendor-bundle Q4'26 (T4) and EU Art 14 enforcement teeth (R5). If both go the wrong way, Branding survives; Process Power survives; downstream Switching attenuates.
- **Bet #2 (Vertical agent role — Hebbia/Sierra/Rogo):** **RENTS** Process Power (Sierra/Hebbia) or Network Economies (Glean) or Cornered Resource (Harvey). Alex's *equity* compounds with the power-holder's; his *personal* Power flavor (Process via operator credibility) accumulates as a side-effect. **Bet#2 is power-rent, not power-build.**
- **Bet #3 (Advisory + gateway-adjacent — Cloudflare/Kong/Pomerium):** Compounds (i) Process Power and (ii) Branding from Bet #1. Pair-with positioning makes Alex the procurement-fluent operator gateways need to close F100 — gateways have Switching Costs (their Power) but no procurement-seam operator. Bet #3 *consumes* Bet #1's Power output.
- **Bet #4 (FinOps / per-trajectory):** Power-thin standalone — fold as Bet#1 module per AI_AGENTS_TRACKER refresh.
- **Bet #5 (Memory + RAG architecture):** Power-thin standalone — Alex doesn't hold Cornered Resource on memory talent. Folds as service-line.
- **Bet #6 (Newsletter):** Distribution layer for Branding flavor. Not a standalone Power but the *flywheel* that compounds (ii) above.
- **Bet #7 (VC platform):** Career fallback. Branding rents, no Power build.

**Verdict.** **Bet #1 has the most-durable Power footing** — three operator-shape flavors stacked, single artifact (Playbook) defends all three, regulatory tailwinds (R5 with teeth) make Branding mandatory not optional. **Bet #5 has the least durable** — memory architecture practice without Cornered Resource on memory talent and without a proprietary substrate is advisory-margin only; Crux #5 directional resolution (consumer absorbed; compliance niche-standalone) already shrinks it. Bets #2 and #3 sit in the middle: #2 rents real Power at high equity cost; #3 compounds Bet #1's Power at advisory margin.

The 7-Powers screen produces a clean sequencing not visible in OCQ or JTBD alone: **Bet #1 (claim the durable Power) → Bet #2 (collect equity at a power-holder) → Bet #3 (compound as practice).** That sequencing matches the OCQ_TRACKER §A refresh and is what F2 contributes specifically to the Phase 5 reconciler.
