# Chapter 0 — Frame

## Why this exists

This volume zooms the agent layer of the AI stack into its own fourteen-sub-stratum view. The parent volume — `output/ai-stack/`, Volumes I and II treated together as the foundation workbook — treats the agent layer as a single band, Stratum XIII, sitting between vertical AI products and end-user surfaces. That single-band treatment is correct at full-stack resolution and wrong at the resolution Alex actually operates against. Three of the seven Bets in V1 Ch 3 sit directly on the agent layer (Bets #2, #3, #4); a fourth (Bet #1) is load-bearing on how F1000 buyers procure agents specifically, not models in the abstract. One band cannot hold four bets without collapsing the distinctions between them.

The zoom is operationally necessary because cycle time inside the agent layer is shorter than at full-stack resolution. V1's bet horizon is 18 months — May 2026 to late 2027 — and that horizon is correct for the full stack. Inside the agent layer, sub-strata move on 12-to-24-month windows: the MCP commons-or-fork question resolves by EOY 2026 (V1 Ch 3 Crux 3), Anthropic ARR resolves by Q3 2026 (V1 Ch 3 Crux 1), OSWorld 65% on a frontier system likely resolves by Q3 2026, EU AI Act Article 14 enforcement teeth show by late 2026. A volume sized to that cadence has to be a different volume.

The consolidation collapses `AI_AGENTS_REPORT.docx`, `AI_AGENTS_ADDENDUM.docx`, `AI_AGENTS_TRACKER.md`, `AI_AGENTS_MASTER_READTHROUGH.docx`, and eleven unreadable matplotlib plates into one editable markdown source plus five SVG plates plus a regenerable EPUB. Same posture as V1: educational density, no aesthetic homage, plates earn their place.

## The thesis (in one sentence)

The highest-leverage agent-layer move for someone with Alex's profile is to plant a flag at the enterprise AI procurement gauntlet — Bet #1, refreshed first in sequence with seven agent-specific overlays — take a vertical-agent GTM seat at one of ten NYC-resident targets (Bet #2), and use the operator-translation newsletter (Bet #6) as the distribution layer that compounds across both — the three positions that the five frameworks converge on once the agent layer is resolved at sub-stratum depth.

## How Volume III composes on Volume I

V1 is the field map; V3 is the higher-resolution zoom of one band. V3 does not re-derive what V1 already concluded — the five framework methods (OCQ, Wardley, 7 Powers, JTBD, Talent & Capital Flow) carry over from V1 Ch 2 verbatim and re-apply to the fourteen agent sub-strata; the 7 Bets, 5 Risks, and 5 Cruxes carry forward from V1 Ch 3 with deltas annotated.

The biggest deltas in V3:

- **Bet #1 sequenced first** — was implicit third in V1; now first on five-framework agent-layer convergence.
- **Bet #3 reframed** — from "build MCP servers" to advisory plus gateway-adjacent; durable power lives at the gateway control plane, not in productized servers.
- **Bet #4 split** — per-token FinOps (V1) and per-trajectory FinOps (V3 reframe); per-trajectory window narrows to ~12 months on hyperscaler auto-routing default-on H2 2027.
- **Bet #5 absorbs memory** — no standalone memory bet; folded as a service line per Crux #5.
- **OSWorld sub-crux** — 65% on a frontier system, feeding V1 Cruxes 2 and 5.

The new artifact in V3 is the Agent-Specific Procurement Rubric — seven overlays (tool-boundary policy, indirect-prompt-injection adaptive red-team, action-rollback documentation, sub-agent privilege separation, signed reproducible eval reports, EU AI Act Article 14 human-oversight tie-out, sectoral overlays) that feed Bet #1's Playbook. Inline summary in Ch 3 §3.5; full text in the Appendix to Bet #1.

The V3 tracker (Ch 4) is a sub-tracker of the V1 tracker (V1 Ch 4). When V3 conviction or status changes, the next V1 monthly ritual (V1 Ch 5 §5.2) mirrors the change back. Reading paths against V1: read V1 first then V3; for agent-specific decisions go straight to V3 Ch 3; for full-stack framing fall back to V1 Ch 1–3.

## How to read this volume

Reading paths analogous to V1's, sized for V3's narrower scope.

- **Full V3 read (~2 hours).** Ch 0 → Ch 6. Use once on first contact and again at each twice-yearly major refresh (V1 Ch 5 §5.4). The order is the reasoning path: agent sub-strata → frameworks → bets → tracker → V3-specific rituals → appendix.
- **Bets refresh only (~25 minutes).** Ch 3 alone. Use during the monthly conviction ritual when an agent-layer signal has moved — Anthropic ARR leak, MCP governance event, OSWorld score, ASL-4 announcement, EU Article 14 draft. Each bet carries a "Delta from V1" subsection so the comparison to the parent framing is one read.
- **Tracker update (~15 minutes monthly).** Ch 4 status tables only. Re-rate the seven bets at agent-layer resolution, log capital events ($50M+ agent-platform rounds), update the NYC snapshot, mark which cruxes have moved.
- **Procurement Rubric drill (~30 minutes when prepping a buyer conversation).** Ch 3 §3.5 plus the Appendix to Bet #1 (full rubric text). Use before any conversation with an F1000 procurement, legal, or InfoSec counterparty about an agent purchase. The rubric IS the conversation structure; the deltas from generic AI procurement are the seven overlays.
- **Onboarding a helper to V3 (~20 minutes).** Ch 0 plus Ch 3. The Frame chapter is short on purpose; pair it with the Bets chapter and a helper has the substrate to operate against without reading the framework chapter or the tracker.

Note the relationship to V1 Ch 5: V3 Ch 5 (Rituals) is intentionally thin. The weekly reflection prompts, monthly conviction ritual, quarterly deep review, and twice-yearly major refresh all live in V1 Ch 5 and govern both volumes. V3 Ch 5 adds only the V3-specific rituals: OSWorld benchmark watch, ASL-4 announcement watch, MCP governance events watch, and a twice-yearly composability audit with Volume IV (GTM) once that lands.
