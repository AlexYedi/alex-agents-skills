# The AI Vendor Procurement Playbook

**A reference for F1000 AI council buyers**

v1.0 · 2026-05-20

---

This playbook is the operationalization of Bet #1 from the AI Agents × GTM workbook (Volume IV). It documents the seven-counterparty F1000 AI procurement gauntlet — InfoSec, Legal, Privacy, AI Governance, Procurement, Business Sponsor, and Enterprise Architecture / IT Ops — and the bilateral evidence-pack interchange format that turns AI vendor diligence from theatre into engineering.

It is written for two audiences. The first: the AI council member or procurement lead trying to land a defensible decision in 90 days without becoming an AI engineer. The second: the AI vendor trying to pass that gauntlet without paying $500K to learn what should be a published spec.

The playbook is open. Distribute freely. The vendor-evidence-pack templates in the appendices are designed to be filled out, signed, and exchanged.

For the underlying analytical work that produced this playbook — the 12 × 13 cell matrix, the framework convergence at cell (9, G), the seven counterparties' specific demands — see `AGENTS_GTM_MASTER.epub` in the parent folder.
