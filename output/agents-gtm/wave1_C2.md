## 7 · THE SINGLE MOST UNDER-SERVED CELL: (5, F) BUYING-COMMITTEE MAPPING

**OCQ 18/20** · **JTBD Job 1 score gap of 7 (Foundation Ecosystem JTBD) · No incumbent owner**

### What someone building this would build (200 words)

The product is **a continuously-refreshed, signed, audit-grade Buying Committee Graph (BCG) for every named target account in an F1000 ABM list** — not a static org chart, not a contact list, not an intent dashboard. Inputs: LinkedIn relationship + role-change signal (via partner, not scrape — TOS is the moat-killer), Common Room–style community signal (GitHub, Slack communities, conference attendance, podcast appearances), email + calendar metadata from M365 / Google Workspace (consented, MCP-mediated), Gong / Chorus call-mentions ("Sarah from Legal joined last call"), internal-CRM relationship history, and a sectoral overlay (which roles sign for which AI-vendor type at which company size). The agent computes a weekly delta: who joined the committee, who left, whose influence shifted, whose buying-intent moved. Output is a **signed evidence artifact** (capability-M tie-in) listing each committee member with role-confidence, last-touch-date, influence-rank, and a "what changed this week" diff. Pricing: per-named-account/month ($50–200 mid-market, $500–1500 F500) — not per-seat. Wedge against Common Room = multi-source signal fusion (CR is community-only); against ZoomInfo / Sales Nav = freshness + diff + signed artifact (both ship static snapshots). Defensibility = signal-fusion engine + signed-artifact format (Bet #1 / capability-M alignment), not the data. **NYC-relevant; under-funded; Alex-network-reachable.**
