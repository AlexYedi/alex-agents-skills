## §5.1 Cell-resolution quarterly add-on (~5 min on top of V1 quarterly)

### When
First Sunday of January, April, July, October — during the V1 quarterly deep review (V1 Ch 5 §5.3). Append to the end of the V1 §5.3 checklist after the V3 delta-audit; do not run separately.

### What you do
Three checks, each ~90 seconds:

1. **Re-rate the top-15 cells** (Ch 1 §1.4). Any cell that crossed Mature → Saturated (incumbent locked in — Vanta in (9, G), MS Copilot for Sales in K-column post-OSWorld 65%) or Forming → Mature (early winner emerged)? Any crossed cell re-rates the bet anchored on it. The (9, G) cell is most-watched — Vanta / Drata / Ironclad shipping fires Bet #1 falsifiability (Ch 3 §3.1).

2. **Cell-coverage audit.** Any "gap" cells (Ch 1 §1.5) that filled with a credible vendor? Any "no incumbent owner" cells that now have one? Leading indicators for Bet #1 timing. A gap that fills before the Playbook publishes Q3 2026 erodes asymmetry; a gap that persists into Q4 2026 reinforces it.

3. **C-bet check.** Any of the 3 promoted Bet-Cs (C5 Common Room, C8 Deal-Diagnosis, C9 Memory-as-Service) ready to promote to a Bet # slot? Any to demote? C5 fires on Common Room roadmap reveal; C8 fires when a credible (6, E) startup emerges; C9 fires at the Q3 2026 trust-earned gate.

### Where it lands
Ch 4 §4.2 and §4.10 (one row per check). Cross-link to V1 Ch 4 §4.10 if any cell-status change re-rates a V1-rated bet.

### What you don't do
Do not rewrite Ch 1 cell content. Do not re-rate bet conviction (V1 monthly ritual). Do not refactor the cell matrix — taxonomy changes are twice-yearly per V1 §5.4.

## §5.2 V4 trigger events (~5 min each, when fired)

Five V4-specific triggers worth a same-day log entry. None requires a chapter rewrite at the moment of the trigger — the rewrite, if needed, happens at the next scheduled monthly or quarterly per V1 Ch 5.

### Trigger A — Outcome-pricing earnings prints

**Fires when** Klarna, Sierra, Decagon, Intercom Fin, Ada, or Hippocratic publish earnings or pricing-model commentary that reveals outcome-pricing economics. Klarna's 2024 reversal (700-agent equivalent → quiet re-hire) is the precedent; analog events fire the same trigger.

**What you do:** Log in Ch 4 §4.8 under Crux #6 (outcome-pricing sustainable or vapor) with date, source, and specific economics (per-resolution, per-task, hybrid). Re-rate Bet #2 — Sierra-Decagon outcome-pricing holding accelerates NYC vertical-agent timing; collapse to seat-based compresses equity bands. Re-rate Bet-C9 — outcome-pricing sustainability is load-bearing for Memory-as-Service productization economics; collapse fires "absorb, do not productize" at the Q3 2026 gate.

### Trigger B — Ironclad AI-vendor-clause-library release

**Fires when** Ironclad, LinkSquares, or SpotDraft ships an AI-vendor-clause library between Apr 2026 and Q4 2026. Bet #1 productization-branch falsifiability test (Ch 3 §3.1).

**What you do:** Log in Ch 4 §4.9 under Risk GR3. Re-rate Bet #1: the productization branch is falsified; the advisory + open-spec-stewardship branch survives because the bilateral evidence-pack interchange format is upstream of any clause library. Conviction delta default: -★ on productization only; standalone Playbook conviction holds. Reconsider Bet-C9 timing — procurement-software incumbents absorbing the layer below changes the trust-earned gate.

### Trigger C — Common Room roadmap reveal

**Fires when** Common Room publishes a roadmap including (or excluding) multi-source signal fusion at the depth Bet-C5 requires. Decidability ~2 quarters out.

**What you do:** Log in Ch 4 §4.8 under Crux GC6. Fusion ships = Bet-C5 JOIN confirmed; promote conviction to ★★★★ pending offer. Fusion omitted = revisit Bet-C1 BUILD path (parked); flag for next quarterly.

### Trigger D — MBB-feed-stock hire events

**Fires when** any new Berger BCG / Park McKinsey / Sinclair Bain → vertical-agent hire posts beyond the three already activated Feb–Apr 2026. Confirms the third career-feed-stock pattern.

**What you do:** Log in Ch 4 §4.3 with date, source, destination, seniority. Re-open the Bet #2 pipeline — each new hire reinforces the cross-introduction graph. Three or more new MBB-network hires in a quarter = "concentrated" signal; Bet #2 sign-window narrows.

### Trigger E — EU AI Act Article 14 enforcement action

**Fires when** the EU Commission issues the first GPAI fine, Article 14 enforcement guidance, or a member-state regulator opens an Article 14 investigation. Horizon: late 2026.

**What you do:** Log under Crux GC3 (Ch 4 §4.8) and Risk GR5 (§4.9). Re-rate Bet #1: teeth = TAM 5× per Wardley PE-2 ($2B niche → $10B+); conviction +★ if the fine is GPAI-scale and cites human-oversight architecture; door closes on horizontal incumbents who cannot retrofit Article 14 tie-out. Paper-tiger = Bet #1 stays niche-but-defensible; advisory leg survives.

## §5.3 Playbook publication ritual (Q3 2026, one-time) (~3 hours total)

This ritual fires ONCE — at Week 12 of Q3 2026, when the Playbook reaches publish-readiness per Ch 3 §3.1 Bet #1 next action. Subsequent versions (v1.1, v2) follow a lighter version of this checklist as needed. Calendar-block the publish target now, before reading on.

### Pre-publish checklist (~3 hours total, week before publish)

1. **Final reconciliation** — confirm the Playbook's seven counterparty chapters in Section II (InfoSec, Legal, Privacy, AI Governance, Procurement, Business Sponsor, EA/IT Ops) align with V3 Ch 3 §3.5 procurement rubric overlays. Mismatches between Playbook and V3 rubric are corrosive — readers will cross-check.

2. **30 expert interviews complete** — 10 buyer-side / 10 seller-side / 10 advisor-side. Each interview produces a 2-paragraph note cross-walked to a specific Playbook section. Interviews complete = bilateral evidence-pack interchange format pressure-tested against named counterparties.

3. **Bilateral evidence-pack interchange format** — confirm as the canonical downloadable. The Wave 1 finding that distinguishes Bet #1 from generic AI-procurement guidance. Published as Appendix to Section II.

4. **Cloudflare gateway-partner conversation opened** — Bet #3a runs concurrent. Open before publish, not after.

5. **Newsletter v1 published** — Bet #6's distribution layer. Issue 1 includes Playbook download CTA above the fold. Metric is conversion from newsletter readers above cold-traffic baseline, not subscriber count.

6. **Email gate live** — single gate, no funnel. Email → downloadable. Do not split-test. The Bet's economics are not the email funnel — they are the 60-day inbound-conversation count.

### Day-of-publish (~90 minutes)

- Post on LinkedIn, X, Hacker News, Lobsters. Different registers per platform — LinkedIn for buyer-side directors; X for Bet #6 operator-translation audience; HN/Lobsters for the gateway-partner audience.
- DM to 30 named NYC procurement leads (AI Governance, InfoSec, AI Council chairs). Personalized, three sentences each.
- Newsletter Issue 1 sends with the Playbook CTA. Cross-pollinate at the gate.
- Open Cloudflare gateway-partner conversation if not already opened.

### 60-day post-publish review

- Downloads (target: 500+); inbound conversations (target: 50+). Bet #1 falsifiability metrics (Ch 3 §3.1). Track weekly, report at day 60.
- Re-rate Bet #1 conviction. Hitting both targets reinforces ★★★★★; missing both fires falsifiability and re-rates to ★★★. Hitting one of two = read inbound quality, not just count.
- Decide on productization branch at month 9. Aligns with Crux GC3 (Article 14) and Crux GC5 (Ironclad bundle) decidability horizons.

This ritual fires ONCE per publication. Subsequent versions (v1.1 quarterly polish, v2 annual refresh) use a lighter version of this checklist.
