# Chapter 4 — Operating Tracker (Agent × GTM Cell Resolution)

This is the V4 living tracker — a sub-tracker of V3's sub-tracker, running at cell-coordinate resolution. The touch-surface discipline carries forward from V1 Ch 4 and V3 Ch 4: bi-weekly talent and capital flow, monthly bet conviction re-rate, trigger-based crux and risk events. The V4 additions are specifically cell-shaped. A monthly cell-velocity check tracks which cells crossed status (Gap → Forming → Mature → Saturated) since the last pass. A quarterly cell-coverage audit re-rates the top-15 cells from Ch 1 §1.4 and asks which gaps filled, which gaps remain. The cell of record — (9, G), the agent procurement gauntlet — is watched continuously: Vanta, Drata, Ironclad, LinkSquares, and SpotDraft product announcements trigger immediate log entries because any of them shipping an AI-vendor bundle re-rates Bet #1's productization branch (Ch 3 §3.1).

Updates here ride alongside V1 + V3 monthly rituals. When a conviction or cell-status changes here, the delta propagates up through V3 Ch 4 §4.2 to V1 Ch 4 §4.2 on the next monthly pass. The V4 ritual add-ons — cell-velocity, cell-coverage audit, V4 trigger events, Playbook publication ritual — live in Ch 5 of this volume; this chapter is the data surface, Ch 5 is the operating procedure.
